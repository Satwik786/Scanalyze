About Scanalyze

Scanalyze is a smart, health-focused product scanner and rating system built to empower consumers with insights into the foods they buy. Whether you're scanning at the store or checking from home, Scanalyze simplifies product information into a clear, easy-to-understand health rating, so you can make informed choices faster.

How It Works

  • Scan a product barcode by uploading a barcode image.
  • Manually enter a barcode if needed.
  • View product information, including:
    • Ingredients
    • Nutritional values
    • Additives and allergens
  • Get a health rating (0-5 stars) based on a smart evaluation of the product's quality and safety.

Who It's For

  • Parents making healthy choices for their kids
  • Seniors managing age-related health concerns
  • People with dietary restrictions (diabetes, nut allergies, gluten intolerance, etc.)
  • Anyone looking to improve their food awareness and make better choices

Our Technology

  • Real-time data from trusted public sources like Open Food Facts
  • A custom rating engine that analyzes key health indicators:
    • Sugar content
    • Harmful additives
    • Allergen presence

Our Mission

We believe that understanding what goes into your food shouldn't be difficult. Scanalyze was created to promote:

  • Safer, smarter consumer decisions
  • Better health awareness
  • Digital inclusion for all ages and backgrounds
