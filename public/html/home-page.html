About Scanalyze

Scanalyze is your smart health companion for food shopping. It instantly scans and analyzes product barcodes to give you a clear, science-backed health rating—helping you make confident choices for yourself and your family, whether you're in the store or at home.

How It Works

  1. Scan a product barcode by uploading an image or entering it manually.
  2. Get a detailed breakdown of:
    • Ingredients and nutritional values
    • Potential allergens and additives
  3. Receive a simple 0–5 star health rating powered by our smart evaluation engine.

Who Benefits

  • Parents seeking healthier options for their children
  • Seniors managing age-related health needs
  • Individuals with dietary restrictions (diabetes, gluten intolerance, nut allergies, etc.)
  • Anyone who wants to shop with greater food awareness

Powered by Technology

  • Real-time, reliable data from trusted public sources like Open Food Facts
  • A custom rating engine that analyzes key health factors:
    • Sugar and nutrient balance
    • Presence of harmful additives
    • Allergen risks

Our Mission

At Scanalyze, we believe understanding your food should be effortless. Our mission is to:

  • Empower smarter, safer consumer decisions
  • Promote better health awareness for everyone
  • Ensure digital accessibility for all ages and backgrounds
