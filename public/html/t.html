Uncovering the truth behind popular Indian packaged products.

“Truth Behind the Label”?

Food packaging often highlights the good parts — like “rich in cocoa,” “made with real fruit,” or “healthy whole grains.” But when you look at the actual ingredients, the story can be very different.

This page uncovers the reality of some popular packaged products in India. By comparing label claims vs. actual contents, we aim to help you make smarter, more informed choices for yourself and your family.

Always check the ingredients list — not just the bold claims on the front!

Kissan Hazelnut Choco Peanut Spread

Label Claim: Contains Hazelnuts

Actual Ingredients: Peanut, Cocoa, Sugar, Vegetable Oil, Milk Solids

Truth: 0% Hazelnut content!

Bournvita Chocolate Malt Drink Mix

Label Claim: Rich in Cocoa and Health Nutrients

Actual Ingredients: Sugar, Malt Extract, Cocoa Powder, Milk Solids, Minerals & Vitamins

Truth: Mostly sugar; very little actual cocoa or nutrients!

Frooti Mango Drink

Label Claim: Mango Drink

Actual Ingredients: Water, Sugar, Mango Pulp (sometimes <10%)

Truth: Contains very little actual mango pulp!

Maggi Masala Noodles

Label Claim: Made with Real Vegetables

Actual Ingredients: Dehydrated vegetable flakes, refined flour, salt, spices

Truth: Only a minimal amount of real vegetables is included!

KurKure Corn Puffs

Label Claim: Corn Snack

Actual Ingredients: Cornmeal, Rice Flour, Starches, Artificial Flavors

Truth: Mostly starch and artificial flavors, not primarily corn!

Tropicana Real Fruit Juice

Label Claim: 100% Real Fruit Juice

Actual Ingredients: Water, Sugar, Fruit Concentrate (often less than 15-20%), Preservatives

Truth: More water and sugar than real fruit!

Kellogg's Chocos Cereal

Label Claim: Made with Whole Grain, Goodness of Wheat

Actual Ingredients: Refined Flour (Maida), Sugar, Cocoa Solids, Very small percentage of whole grain

Truth: Mostly sugar and refined flour, not really “healthy whole grain”!

Remember!

Tip: Ingredients are usually listed in order of quantity. The first 2-3 items make up most of the product — so if that's sugar, flour, or oil, you know what you're really consuming.
